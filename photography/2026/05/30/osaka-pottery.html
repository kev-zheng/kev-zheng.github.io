Osaka Pottery 2026 🏺

May 30, 2026



Ceramics inspiration in Osaka.

  • Osaka Ceramics Museum had a lovely collection of ancient to modern pieces.
  • I had booked Kaminoza to treat my friends to a nice sounding omakase experience while on vacation. It was both delicious and, unexpectedly, a feast for the eyes, in terms of the amount of pieces of dinnerware created by independent artists in Osaka. The group of us four probably ate out of over 100 unique pieces during this meal.
